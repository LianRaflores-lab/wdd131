ABOUT US

Antipolo Pickleball Club is a welcoming community for players of all ages and skill levels. Our mission is to promote the sport of pickleball in Antipolo by organizing games, clinics, tournaments, and social events. Whether you're picking up a paddle for the first time or you're an experienced player, you'll find a friendly environment to learn, compete, and build lasting friendships.

OUR MISSION

Making Pickleball Accessible to Everyone

Our goal is to make pickleball accessible to everyone in Antipolo. By hosting community events, providing beginner-friendly resources, and connecting local players, we hope to inspire more people to discover and enjoy this exciting sport.
